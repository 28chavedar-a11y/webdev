History

Los Tapatios (specifically the taco shop variant) began in 2020 as a family-owned business in Universal City, Texas, focused on authentic birria recipes from Jalisco, Mexico. Founded by brothers, it expanded to locations in Utah (Taylorsville/Salt Lake City) and features a signature beef birria.
